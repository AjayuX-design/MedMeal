RT and PEG feeding

When a patient cannot eat enough by mouth, a feeding tube can give nutrition. MedMeal makes feeds for both tube types.

RT / Ryles tube

A feeding tube that passes through the nose into the stomach. It can be used for enteral feeding when clinically indicated.

MedMeal provides specialized nutrition support for patients fed through an RT/Ryles tube.

PEG tube

A feeding tube placed through the abdominal wall to reach the stomach.

MedMeal provides nutrition support for patients fed through a PEG tube, based on their nutritional needs.
