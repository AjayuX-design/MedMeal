Questions from hospitals

Which hospitals and care centres does MedMeal work with?

We serve 9 hospitals and care centres in and around Vijayawada. Send us an enquiry and we will talk through what your team needs.

What tube feeds can you supply?

RT (Ryles) and PEG feeds, feeds for neuro, oncology, gastro, renal and cardiac conditions and surgical recovery, plus semi-solid and therapeutic weaning feeds.

Who plans the feeds?

Our dietitians plan each feed around the patient's condition and needs.

Can you deliver to patients at home after discharge?

Yes, within our service areas. Call or message us to check the address.
