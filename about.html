About MedMeal

Nutrition support for patients who cannot eat normally, planned with dietitians and delivered with care.

Nutrition should be part of recovery, not an afterthought.

MedMeal began as a personal realization. As a neurosurgeon, Dr. Ameen spent countless hours in intensive care units. He saw that patients who cannot swallow depend on precise, timely feeding to recover.

He also saw that many patients struggle to get proper nutritional care after leaving the hospital. MedMeal was created to close that gap.

Dr. Mohammad A. AmeenFounder, MedMeal

How we work
