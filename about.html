Our story

MedMeal began with a simple idea: patients deserve better nutrition while they recover. Today we are a clinical nutrition service in Vijayawada, making tube feeding (enteral nutrition) for patients fed through an RT (Ryles) tube or a PEG tube.

01

Why we started

Families caring for a patient on tube feeding find it hard to get the ingredients, hygiene and nutrition right, and every step counts.

02

What we built

One service that brings together dietitian guidance, hygienic preparation in our Nutrition Lab and convenient delivery.

03

Where we are today

Specialized feeds for different medical needs, supporting patients in hospitals, rehabilitation centres and at home across Vijayawada.

Questions about tube feeding

What is MedMeal?

MedMeal is a clinical nutrition service in Vijayawada. We make dietitian-planned tube feeding food for RT and PEG patients.

Is MedMeal FSSAI licensed?

Yes. Our FSSAI licence number is 10126020000188, valid until 10 March 2027.

Who plans the feeds?

Dietitians plan each feed around the patient's condition and needs.

Do you deliver tube feeding food in Vijayawada?

Yes, we deliver to homes, hospitals and care centres within our service areas. Call or message us to check your address.

Can hospitals work with MedMeal?

Yes. We already serve 9 hospitals and care centres. Send us an enquiry about feeds and delivery.
